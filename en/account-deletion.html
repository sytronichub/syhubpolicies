Account Deletion Agreement

Last updated: 15 November 2026

This Account Deletion Agreement (the "Agreement") is a supplementary agreement between you and Sytronic Hub ("we", "us" or the "Platform") regarding the deletion of a Sytronic Hub account. Deletion is an irreversible and significant operation; please read this Agreement in full before submitting your request and confirm that you have backed up the content you need to keep and settled the relevant matters.

By submitting a deletion request, completing identity verification or clicking to confirm deletion, you confirm that you have understood and agreed to this Agreement. If you do not agree, please do not submit a deletion request.

1. Scope of Application and Definitions

This Agreement applies to your voluntary application to delete a Sytronic Hub account, as well as the data processing, content display, virtual benefits, binding relationships and subsequent effects arising from deletion. This Agreement forms part of the User Service Agreement and applies together with the Privacy Policy, the Community Guidelines and the Minor Service Agreement; where this Agreement makes special provision on deletion matters, that provision shall prevail.

"Deletion" means the act by which the Platform, in accordance with its procedures, disables the account's login capability, stops account-related services and processes the relevant data in accordance with this Agreement and the Privacy Policy. Deletion does not mean the deletion of all records that the Platform is required to retain by law, nor does it mean the discharge of liability already incurred before deletion.

2. Conditions for Deletion

  • You shall be the lawful holder of the right to use the account and be able to pass the identity verification required by the Platform; where necessary, the Platform may require verification by email, mobile phone number, password, passkey, device or other means.
  • The account has no unfinished transactions, unsettled amounts, unprocessed refunds, incomplete withdrawals, unresolved reports/appeals/disputes or other matters that the Platform considers need to be resolved first.
  • No circumstance exists in which laws and regulations, regulatory requirements or judicial or administrative authorities require retention or restrict deletion of the account.
  • The account is not frozen, misappropriated, subject to ownership disputes, subject to identity impersonation or otherwise subject to security risks; where risks exist, the Platform may require verification or security handling to be completed first.
  • Where a minor applies for deletion, the guardian's consent shall be obtained and verification of the guardianship relationship shall be completed as required by the Platform.

3. Deletion Process

  • Submit the request: you may access the deletion page in account settings, read the risk warnings and submit your request.
  • Identity verification: the Platform will verify account ownership, binding information and security status; if verification fails, the deletion process may be suspended.
  • Cooling-off period: to prevent mistaken operations, a cooling-off period may be set for deletion. During the cooling-off period you may withdraw your request in accordance with the page instructions; when the cooling-off period expires, the Platform will execute the deletion.
  • Execution of deletion: once deletion is completed, the account can no longer be logged in, and the account profile, content and features generally cannot be restored. The Platform will inform you of the outcome through the page or a notice.
  • Suspension in exceptional circumstances: if transaction disputes, violation investigations, judicial/regulatory requirements, account misappropriation or other anomalies are discovered, the Platform is entitled to suspend or terminate the deletion process and notify you for handling.

4. Consequences of Deletion

  • Login, publishing, commenting, direct messaging, favourites, following, messages, the Creator Centre, live streaming, Particles, the Pioneer Programme and other features of the account will become unavailable.
  • The account's profile page will generally no longer display videos, image-text posts, updates and other content published by the account; the relevant content may be deleted, anonymised, hidden or retained only in accordance with law.
  • Content published by the account may continue to be retained, deleted or otherwise handled as required by laws and regulations, copyright requirements, requests of third-party rights holders, community governance or dispute resolution.
  • The mobile phone number, email address, third-party accounts, devices, real-name verification information and the like bound to the account may continue to be retained in accordance with laws and regulations and the Platform's security, audit and anti-fraud needs; this may affect your subsequent registration or binding of other accounts using the same binding information.
  • Particles, memberships, decorations, subscriptions, revenue, coupons, favourites capacity and other virtual benefits in the account will, unless otherwise provided by law or expressly promised by the Platform, lapse after deletion and will not be refunded or compensated.
  • Account deletion does not affect legal liability already incurred before deletion, the rights the Platform enjoys under the agreement, the handling of violations, or records that must be retained in accordance with law.

5. Data Processing and Retention

We will process your personal information in accordance with the Privacy Policy. After deletion, we will delete or anonymise your personal information, except for information that must be retained as required by laws and regulations or for the purposes of handling disputes, security auditing, anti-fraud, financial settlement or handling reports. Information retained in accordance with law will be subject to secure isolation measures and used only for statutory purposes and within statutory periods.

The following information may be retained in accordance with law: network logs, real-name verification records, transaction and settlement records, invoice and tax materials, report and penalty records, account security and abnormal login records, and information required to be retained by judicial or administrative authorities. After the retention period expires, we will delete or anonymise the information.

6. Recommendations Before Deletion

  • Back up the videos, image-text posts, comments, direct messages, favourites and creative materials you need to keep.
  • Settle unfinished orders, refunds, withdrawals, Particle settlements, creator revenue and subscriptions.
  • Unbind third-party accounts you no longer need and resolve pending reports, appeals or disputes.
  • Confirm that you have read and understood the irreversible consequences of deletion.

7. Minors

Where a minor applies to delete an account, the guardian's consent shall be obtained in advance, and verification of identity and the guardianship relationship shall be completed as required by the Platform. Guardians shall assist minors in backing up necessary content, handling purchases and binding relationships, and understanding the consequences of deletion.

8. Updates to This Agreement

We may update this Agreement based on laws and regulations, regulatory requirements or product changes. The updated Agreement will be published in the Policy Centre with its effective date indicated; material changes will be notified in an appropriate manner. After the update takes effect, your continued use of the Platform's services constitutes acceptance of the updated Agreement.

9. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Agreement or account deletion, you may contact us through "Feedback and Suggestions", the Help Centre or help@sytronichub.com.
