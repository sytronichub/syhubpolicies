Community Guidelines

Last updated: 15 November 2026
Version history:

Welcome to Sytronic Hub. We hope this will be a community where creators and viewers can express themselves, communicate and support one another with peace of mind. These Community Guidelines apply to the content you publish on the Platform, your comments, danmaku, direct messages, updates, live streams, profile information and all interactive behavior.

These Guidelines form part of the User Service Agreement. Violation of these Guidelines may result in measures such as content handling, feature restrictions, muting, bans, freezing of revenue and account deletion; where a violation is suspected of being illegal or criminal, we may report it to the relevant authorities in accordance with law.

1. Basic Principles

  • Respect others: be courteous and empathetic; personal attacks, insults, discrimination, harassment, threats, cyberbullying and doxxing are prohibited.
  • Express yourself truthfully: do not impersonate others, institutions, official accounts or original characters, and do not forge identities, qualifications, authorizations, transaction records or supporting materials.
  • Comply with the law: comply with the laws and regulations of your place of residence and the Platform Rules, and do not publish or disseminate illegal or harmful information.
  • Respect copyright: obtain lawful authorization or meet the conditions for fair use before reposting or re-uploading content, editing it, adding music, or using fonts and images.
  • Protect privacy: do not disclose others' real names, addresses, telephone numbers, ID numbers, whereabouts, accounts, chat records or other identifiable information.
  • Protect minors: do not publish sexualised, abusive, inducement-related, dangerous or private content involving minors.

2. Accounts and Profile Information

2.1 Account Profile

Avatars, nicknames, bios, cover images, tags and linked accounts may not contain content that is illegal, pornographic, violent, insulting, discriminatory, advertising or traffic-diverting, falsely official, impersonating others, or infringing trademarks or copyright. QR codes, contact details, external links or other means may not be used to circumvent on-site promotion rules.

2.2 Account Behavior

You may not buy, sell, lease, lend or share accounts, may not bulk register or use device farms, scripts or emulators to inflate metrics, and may not misappropriate others' accounts, verification codes, passkeys or login credentials. Where an account is abnormal, the Platform may require additional verification or temporarily freeze it.

2.3 Real-Name and Age

Users in mainland China shall complete real-name verification as required. No one may use another person's documents, forge documents, synthesise faces, use deepfakes, impersonate identities or circumvent age restrictions. Minors may not bypass guardians' or the Platform's minor protection measures.

2.4 Original Characters and Linked Accounts

Original character accounts shall clearly indicate the character's attributes and may not impersonate real persons, institutions or official accounts. Character settings, avatars, bios and content may not infringe others' intellectual property rights, portrait rights, reputation rights or privacy rights.

3. Content Standards

3.1 Politics and Public Events

Please comply with laws and regulations, and do not produce, publish or disseminate content that endangers national security, undermines ethnic solidarity, promotes terrorism or extremism, incites ethnic hatred, undermines religious policy, spreads political rumours or maliciously interprets public events. Discussion of public events shall be based on facts, and you should avoid inciting confrontation, spreading unverified information or attacking particular groups.

3.2 Pornography, Vulgarity and Sexual Suggestiveness

It is prohibited to publish obscene, pornographic, child pornographic, sexually exploitative, sexually violent, sex-work-related, secretly filmed, voyeuristic, excessively sexually suggestive, vulgar and titillating content, or sexualised content involving minors. Content in special contexts such as art, medicine and education shall also be presented in a necessary, restrained and non-titillating manner, and shall be marked as restricted where required.

3.3 Violence, Danger and Self-Harm

It is prohibited to promote violence, terrorism, animal abuse, self-harm, suicide, imitation of dangerous acts, extreme challenges, gory imagery or other content that may cause personal injury. Where necessary contexts such as news, documentary and education are involved, prominent warnings, mosaics or restrictions on dissemination shall be applied.

3.4 Illegal and Dangerous Items

It is prohibited to disseminate information about gambling, drugs, firearms and ammunition, controlled knives, explosives, hazardous chemicals, counterfeit currency, forged documents, hacking tools, malware, phishing links, fraud methods, pyramid selling, illegal fundraising, money laundering, illegal lending or other illegal information.

3.5 Hatred, Discrimination and Harassment

It is prohibited to insult, discriminate against, express hatred towards, exclude, threaten or incite violence against others on the basis of ethnicity, race, nationality, religion, gender, sexual orientation, gender identity, age, disability, illness, regional origin or other protected characteristics. It is prohibited to organise or participate in cyberbullying, doxxing, malicious exposure, persistent harassment or retaliation.

3.6 Privacy and Personal Information

Without consent, you may not disclose others' real names, likenesses, addresses, telephone numbers, ID numbers, license plates, whereabouts, workplaces, chat records, financial information, health information or other identifiable information. In circumstances involving the public interest, such as news reporting and public oversight, the principles of necessity and minimisation shall also be followed.

3.7 False Information and Fraud

It is prohibited to fabricate or disseminate fake news, rumours, forged evidence, fake prize schemes, part-time job order-brushing scams, investment and wealth management scams, customer service impersonation, impersonation of public security and judicial organs, false medical and health information or other fraudulent content. Content that may mislead others shall be prominently labelled with its source, context and uncertainty.

3.8 Advertising and Traffic Diversion

Without the Platform's permission, you may not publish commercial advertisements, solicitation information, QR codes, contact details, external links or traffic-diverting content. Authorised commercial content shall be prominently labelled "advertisement" or "sponsored", and may not involve false advertising, exaggerated claims, misleading consumers or circumvention of advertising review.

3.9 AI-Generated, Fictional and Risky Content

Those who create content using AI generation, deep synthesis, face swapping, voice dubbing or virtual avatars shall comply with laws and regulations and provide prominent labels, and may not fabricate real persons, events, evidence or identities, or infringe others' likeness, voice, reputation or copyright. Fictional performances, dangerous actions and controversial content shall be labelled as required by the Platform.

4. Interaction, Promotion and Reporting

4.1 Flame Wars, Spamming and Comment Manipulation

It is prohibited to organise, incite or participate in flame wars, abusive language, spamming, comment manipulation, malicious liking/disliking, bulk repetitive commenting, malicious occupation of comment sections, or harassment of creators or other users. Different opinions shall be expressed rationally, and you may not suppress others' expression through reporting, negative reviews or group attacks.

4.2 Promotion under Others' Content

Without permission, you may not post advertisements, traffic diversion, recruitment, QR codes, contact details or promotional information unrelated to the content in others' videos, image-text posts, updates or comment sections. Normal cooperation between creators shall be disclosed through means permitted by the Platform.

4.3 Malicious Reporting

Reports shall be based on true facts and reasonable grounds. False reporting, bulk reporting, retaliatory reporting, use of reporting to attack competitors or interference with the Platform's normal review are prohibited. Abuse of reporting may result in restriction of the reporting feature or account features.

4.4 Interaction Etiquette

Please respect different viewpoints and creative styles, and avoid personal attacks, regional discrimination, gender discrimination, age discrimination, illness-related discrimination or other offensive expressions. Minors, victims and vulnerable groups shall be given special protection.

5. Re-Uploading, Copyright and Use of Content

5.1 Re-Uploading and Derivative Works

Before reposting or re-uploading content, editing it, translating it, adding music, or using fonts/images/film or television clips, you shall obtain the rights holder's authorization or meet the conditions for fair use. You may not remove watermarks, tamper with ownership information, pass off content as original or bulk re-upload others' works. Derivative works shall credit the source materials and the original author, and may not infringe the original author's lawful rights and interests.

5.2 Copyright and Regional Restrictions

Content subject to copyright, contractual or regional legal restrictions may be visible only in specific regions, at specific times or to specific users. You may not use VPNs, proxies, location modification, screen recording and dissemination or other means to circumvent copyright and regional restrictions.

5.3 Infringement Complaints

Where a rights holder believes that content is infringing, they may submit proof of ownership, the infringing link and a specific description through the reporting channel or help@sytronichub.com. The Platform will handle the matter in accordance with law; those who file malicious or false complaints or abuse the complaint process may bear the corresponding liability.

6. Handling of Violations and the Black Room

6.1 Handling Principles

The Platform adopts graduated and progressive measures based on the nature, circumstances and impact of the violation, subjective fault, whether it is repeated, whether the user cooperates with rectification and whether damage is caused. Measures include content deletion/blocking, warnings, restriction of comments/uploads/direct messages/live streaming/search/recommendations, suspension of revenue, temporary muting, temporary bans, permanent bans, device restrictions, account deletion and reporting in accordance with law.

6.2 Penalty Levels

  • Level 1 (minor): for a first minor violation that is corrected promptly and has limited impact, measures such as content deletion, a warning or restriction of features for 1–7 days may be taken.
  • Level 2 (general): repeated minor violations, flame wars and spamming, advertising and traffic diversion, privacy infringement, re-uploading infringing content, circumvention of review and the like may result in restriction of uploads/comments/direct messages for 7–30 days, suspension of revenue or removal of the offending content.
  • Level 3 (serious): pornography or vulgarity, violence and gore, fraud, gambling, malware, organising cyberbullying, buying or selling accounts, forging identity, circumventing real-name/regional restrictions and the like may result in bans of 30 days or more, permanent bans, device restrictions or freezing of the relevant benefits.
  • Level 4 (particularly serious): endangering national security, terrorism, child pornography, major fraud, organised crime, serious infringement of minors' rights and interests and the like will result in an immediate permanent ban, preservation of evidence and reporting to the relevant authorities in accordance with law.

6.3 Publication in the Black Room

For accounts that are muted, banned or otherwise penalised, the Platform may publish the account identifier, the type of violation, the time and duration of the penalty and the appeal status on the "Black Room" or a similar page. Publication shall be limited to what is necessary and shall not display sensitive personal information unrelated to the handling of the violation. Once the penalty period expires or an appeal succeeds, the Platform will lift or adjust the publication in accordance with the rules.

6.4 Appeals

If you object to a penalty, you may submit an appeal through the appeal channel in the penalty notice or through customer service, explaining your reasons and providing evidence. The Platform will review the matter and respond within a reasonable period. An appeal does not automatically suspend enforcement of the penalty; malicious, repetitive or false appeals may be restricted.

6.5 Cooperation with Investigations

To investigate violations, handle complaints, ensure security or perform legal obligations, the Platform may require explanations, supporting materials or cooperation with identity verification. Refusal to cooperate, destruction of evidence, forgery of materials or continued violations may result in aggravated measures.

7. Protection of Minors

Minors shall use the Platform under the guidance of their guardians and may not access or publish content that laws and regulations prohibit them from accessing. The Platform may restrict minors' access to live streaming, payments, tipping, direct messages, personalized recommendations or some content.

If you discover sexualisation, abuse, inducement, fraud, privacy leakage or dangerous behavior involving minors, please report it immediately. The Platform will give priority to such reports and, where necessary, preserve evidence and report to the relevant authorities in accordance with law.

8. Reporting and Feedback

You may report non-compliant content, accounts or interactions through the reporting channel on the content page, the Help Center or help@sytronichub.com. Please provide information that is as specific as possible (link, screenshot, time, account, type of violation) and ensure that the report is truthful. The Platform will keep reporters' information confidential and handle the matter in accordance with law.

If you object to the Platform's handling outcome, you may provide feedback through the appeal channel. We welcome community users' suggestions regarding our rules and products.

9. Updates to These Guidelines

We may revise these Guidelines based on laws and regulations, community governance needs and product changes. The revised Guidelines will be published in the Policy Center with their effective date indicated; material changes will be notified through in-site announcements or other reasonable means. Continued use of the Platform constitutes acceptance of the revised Guidelines.

10. Contact Us

If you have any questions, comments or complaints about these Guidelines, please contact help@sytronichub.com. We will handle and respond within a reasonable period.

Welcome to Sytronic Hub. We are committed to an open, inclusive and friendly environment. Please read and follow these Community Guidelines.

1. Basic Principles

  • Respect others: be polite and empathetic; no personal attacks, insults, discrimination or harassment.
  • Speak civilly: no vulgar, hateful or threatening language.
  • Keep content lawful: no content that violates the laws of the People’s Republic of China.

1.1 Mainland China Real-Name Verification

For users in mainland China, local laws require real-name verification. You may complete it on the Platform or through our WeChat Mini Program. Users who have not completed verification may be unable to publish, comment or take part in community activities.

2. Content Rules

2.1 Political Content

Political content is prohibited in any form, stance or expression, including text, images, audio, video, comments, posts or other content concerning political positions, figures, events, policy disputes or ideology. Such content will be removed and accounts may be restricted or banned.

2.2 Restricted Content Label

Restricted content must be labeled as “Restricted Content” when published. Unlabeled content is treated as a violation and may be returned, removed or restricted; the publisher bears the consequences.

2.3 Other Prohibited Content

  • Pornography, nudity, sexual suggestion or vulgar content.
  • Violence, gore, terrorism or incitement to violence.
  • False information, rumours or misleading content.
  • Content infringing copyright, trademark or privacy.
  • Gambling, drugs or illegal transactions.

2.4 Cults and Proselytizing

Cults and proselytizing are prohibited, including promoting or spreading cult or superstitious organizations, or recruiting or soliciting in any form.

3. Interaction, Promotion and Reporting

3.1 Flaming and Spamming

No flaming, provocation, incitement, spamming, comment flooding or bullet-screen flooding. Confirmed violations may lead to content removal or account bans.

3.2 Advertising under Others’ Works

No advertising under others’ works, especially illegal or high-risk ads such as fraud, gambling, pornography or traffic diversion. Content will be removed and accounts banned.

3.3 Malicious Reporting

Malicious reporting, false accusations or abuse of reporting are prohibited. Confirmed malicious reporters will be banned.

4. Re-uploading and Copyright

4.1 Re-uploading Works

Unauthorized re-uploading of others’ works will result in the work being returned; serious cases may lead to account bans. Original creators may apply with proof to have the work attributed to their account.

4.2 Copyright-Restricted Content

Where content is subject to copyright restrictions, we may impose regional restrictions or, where necessary, restrict display or remove it at the rights holder’s request or based on risk assessment.

5. Reporting and Handling

Report violations through the Platform’s reporting feature or help@sytronichub.com. We will review and take appropriate measures, including removal, warnings, feature restrictions or account bans.

6. Enforcement

  • Minor violation: remove content and issue a warning.
  • Moderate violation: restrict features such as commenting or publishing for a period.
  • Serious violation: permanently ban the account and reserve legal rights.

7. Updates

We may revise these Guidelines. The updated version will be published; continued use after it takes effect means acceptance.

8. Contact

help@sytronichub.com.
