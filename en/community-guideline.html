Welcome to Sytronic Hub. We are committed to an open, inclusive and friendly environment. Please read and follow these Community Guidelines.

1. Basic Principles

  • Respect others: be polite and empathetic; no personal attacks, insults, discrimination or harassment.
  • Speak civilly: no vulgar, hateful or threatening language.
  • Keep content lawful: no content that violates the laws of the People’s Republic of China.

1.1 Mainland China Real-Name Verification

For users in mainland China, local laws require real-name verification. You may complete it on the Platform or through our WeChat Mini Program. Users who have not completed verification may be unable to publish, comment or take part in community activities.

2. Content Rules

2.1 Political Content

Political content is prohibited in any form, stance or expression, including text, images, audio, video, comments, posts or other content concerning political positions, figures, events, policy disputes or ideology. Such content will be removed and accounts may be restricted or banned.

2.2 Restricted Content Label

Restricted content must be labeled as “Restricted Content” when published. Unlabeled content is treated as a violation and may be returned, removed or restricted; the publisher bears the consequences.

2.3 Other Prohibited Content

  • Pornography, nudity, sexual suggestion or vulgar content.
  • Violence, gore, terrorism or incitement to violence.
  • False information, rumours or misleading content.
  • Content infringing copyright, trademark or privacy.
  • Gambling, drugs or illegal transactions.

2.4 Cults and Proselytizing

Cults and proselytizing are prohibited, including promoting or spreading cult or superstitious organizations, or recruiting or soliciting in any form.

3. Interaction, Promotion and Reporting

3.1 Flaming and Spamming

No flaming, provocation, incitement, spamming, comment flooding or bullet-screen flooding. Confirmed violations may lead to content removal or account bans.

3.2 Advertising under Others’ Works

No advertising under others’ works, especially illegal or high-risk ads such as fraud, gambling, pornography or traffic diversion. Content will be removed and accounts banned.

3.3 Malicious Reporting

Malicious reporting, false accusations or abuse of reporting are prohibited. Confirmed malicious reporters will be banned.

4. Re-uploading and Copyright

4.1 Re-uploading Works

Unauthorized re-uploading of others’ works will result in the work being returned; serious cases may lead to account bans. Original creators may apply with proof to have the work attributed to their account.

4.2 Copyright-Restricted Content

Where content is subject to copyright restrictions, we may impose regional restrictions or, where necessary, restrict display or remove it at the rights holder’s request or based on risk assessment.

5. Reporting and Handling

Report violations through the Platform’s reporting feature or help@sytronichub.com. We will review and take appropriate measures, including removal, warnings, feature restrictions or account bans.

6. Enforcement

  • Minor violation: remove content and issue a warning.
  • Moderate violation: restrict features such as commenting or publishing for a period.
  • Serious violation: permanently ban the account and reserve legal rights.

7. Updates

We may revise these Guidelines. The updated version will be published; continued use after it takes effect means acceptance.

8. Contact

help@sytronichub.com.
