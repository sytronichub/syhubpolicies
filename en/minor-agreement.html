Minors' Service Agreement

Last updated: 15 November 2026
Version history:

This Minors' Service Agreement (the "Agreement") applies to the use of Sytronic Hub services by users under the age of eighteen. Before using the Platform, minors shall read this Agreement together with their guardians and use the Platform with their guardians' knowledge, consent and guidance. If you are under the age of fourteen, you shall also comply with the special provisions of applicable laws and regulations on the protection of children's personal information.

This Agreement forms part of the User Service Agreement and applies together with the Privacy Policy, the Community Guidelines and the Account Deletion Agreement. The Platform may impose stricter restrictions on certain features, content and interactions as needed for the protection of minors.

1. Restricted Content and Age Notices

The Platform contains restricted content uploaded by users, which may not be suitable for minors to consult or view. The Platform will, in accordance with its rules, label restricted content, restrict its display or require age confirmation; minors should not actively search for, view or disseminate unsuitable content, or circumvent restrictions to access it.

The Platform may apply stricter review, mosaics, removal or dissemination restrictions to content involving violence, gore, terror, sexual suggestiveness, dangerous behavior, gambling, drugs, fraud or other content unsuitable for minors.

2. Guardian Awareness and Consent

Guardians of minors should understand the purpose, duration, content and spending involved in minors' use of the Platform, and should guide, supervise and reasonably restrict minors' use. Minors shall obtain guardian consent before registering, binding accounts, using community features, making payments or tipping.

Guardians should properly manage payment accounts, devices, app store passwords and minor mode to prevent minors from making top-ups, tipping, subscriptions or purchases of virtual benefits without consent.

3. Usage Recommendations

  • Use the Platform under the guidance of your guardian, arrange your usage time reasonably and avoid affecting your studies, rest and physical and mental health.
  • Do not disclose your real name, address, school, class, contact details, ID number, whereabouts or other personal information to strangers.
  • Do not readily believe strangers' messages about transfers, part-time jobs, order brushing, investment, prizes, loans, threats or inducements.
  • Do not participate in cyberbullying, doxxing, malicious reporting, flame wars, spamming or other harmful interactions.
  • When you encounter unsuitable content, harassment, inducement, fraud or dangerous behavior, you should immediately stop using the service, preserve evidence and inform your guardian, and at the same time provide feedback through the Platform's reporting feature.
  • Do not make top-ups, tips, subscriptions, transactions or meet offline without your guardian's consent.

4. Access to Restricted Content

For restricted content that has been labelled, the Platform may require users to confirm their age, log in, complete real-name verification or apply other access restrictions. Minors should not circumvent the age confirmation, access restrictions, guardian controls or minor mode set by the Platform.

The Platform is entitled to restrict minors' accounts from live streaming, payments, tipping, direct messages, personalized recommendations, access to some content or other features in order to protect minors' rights and interests.

5. Personal Information and Privacy Protection

The Platform values the protection of minors' personal information, collects and uses information only to the extent necessary to provide the Services and processes it in accordance with the Privacy Policy. For children under the age of fourteen, the Platform will obtain guardian consent or rely on another lawful basis in accordance with laws and regulations.

If a guardian discovers that a minor's information has been improperly collected, used, disclosed or abused, the guardian may contact us through the means set out at the end of this Agreement to request access, correction, deletion or other protective measures.

6. Spending and Virtual Benefits

Minors shall obtain guardian consent before making payments, tipping, subscribing or purchasing virtual benefits such as Particles. Where a guardian discovers that a minor has spent money without consent, the guardian may submit supporting materials to Platform customer service to apply for handling; the Platform will verify and handle the matter in accordance with laws and regulations and the Platform Rules.

Minors may not use the Platform to engage in gambling, cash-out, money laundering, fraud, illegal transactions or other unlawful activities, and may not misuse guardians' identities or payment accounts.

7. Account and Content Handling

If a minor violates laws and regulations, this Agreement or the Platform Rules, the Platform is entitled, in accordance with law and this Agreement, to take measures such as restricting features, deleting content, suspending or terminating services, and notifying the guardian. Guardians shall cooperate with the Platform in handling the relevant matters.

Where the Platform discovers that a minor has suffered abuse, inducement, fraud, cyberbullying, privacy leakage or other dangerous situations, it may give priority to handling the matter, preserve evidence, restrict the relevant accounts and report to the relevant authorities in accordance with law.

8. Updates to This Agreement and Contact

We may update this Agreement based on laws and regulations, regulatory requirements or product changes. The updated Agreement will be published in the Policy Center with its effective date indicated; material changes will be notified in an appropriate manner. If you have any questions about this Agreement, you may contact us through "Feedback and Suggestions", the Help Center or help@sytronichub.com.
