Sytronic Hub ("we", "us" or the "Platform") understands the importance of personal information to you. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, share, transfer and protect your personal information when you use the Sytronic Hub website, mobile applications, desktop client, embedded pages and other related services, and how you may exercise your related rights.

Please read this Policy carefully before using the Services, in particular the provisions on sensitive personal information, third-party sharing, cross-border transfer, protection of minors and limitation of liability. By clicking agree, registering, logging in or actually using the Services, you confirm that you understand this Policy. If you do not agree, please stop using the relevant features.

This Policy applies to Sytronic Hub's own products and services and does not apply to services independently operated by third parties. Third-party services are subject to their own privacy policies.

1. Information We Collect

1.1 Account and Identity Information

  • Registration, login and account management information: username, nickname, UID, password (stored encrypted), email address, mobile phone number, country/region, registration IP, registration time and account status;
  • Third-party login information: identifiers, nicknames, avatars and authorization scopes obtained from third parties such as Google, GitHub, Discord and WeChat after your authorization;
  • Real-name verification information: real name, document type, document number, document photograph, date of birth, nationality/region, facial verification results, verification status and reasons for rejection;
  • Profile information: avatar, cover image, bio, gender, birthday, region, linked accounts, original character information and other information you voluntarily provide.

1.2 Content and Interaction Information

  • the videos, image-text posts, updates, comments, danmaku, subtitles, covers, favorites, likes, follows, reports, appeals and direct messages you publish;
  • records of your interactions with creators, other users and customer service;
  • the files, images, audio and video you upload and the metadata contained therein (such as shooting time and device information, depending on the file).

1.3 Device, Log and Network Information

  • device model, operating system, browser type, client version, screen resolution, language and time zone;
  • IP address, approximate/precise location (only within the scope of your authorization or as permitted by law), network type and carrier;
  • access time, pages visited, click records, search records, playback records, dwell time, crash logs, error logs and performance data;
  • device identifiers, login devices, session information and records of abnormal behavior used for security risk control.

1.4 Payment and Virtual Benefit Information

  • order number, product or service name, amount, payment channel, payment status, refund records and invoice information;
  • Particle balance, Particle transaction history, support records, Pioneer Program, subscription status and records of virtual benefit usage;
  • payments are processed by third-party payment institutions, and we generally do not directly collect your full bank card number, payment password or CVV; the relevant payment information is processed by the payment institution in accordance with its privacy policy.

1.5 Customer Service, Reporting and Appeal Information

When you contact customer service, or submit a report, appeal, account recovery request, data request or complaint, we collect the contact details, problem description, supporting materials, communication records and handling results you provide.

1.6 Cookies and Similar Technologies

We use cookies, LocalStorage, pixels, SDK logs and similar technologies to maintain login status, remember preferences, ensure security, compile visit statistics and improve the Services. You may manage cookies through your browser or system settings, but disabling essential cookies may render some features unavailable.

2. How We Use Information

We use your personal information only for the following purposes and to the extent necessary:

  • creating and managing accounts, and completing login, identity verification, account recovery, device management and account switching;
  • completing real-name verification, age verification, anti-fraud, anti-spam, content security and regional compliance requirements;
  • providing features such as content publishing, recommendation, search, playback, comments, danmaku, direct messages, favorites, following, live streaming and the Creator Center;
  • processing orders, payments, Particles, the Pioneer Program, subscriptions, refunds, settlements and customer service requests;
  • ensuring service security and detecting, preventing and handling fraud, attacks, metric inflation, malicious registration, non-compliant content and security incidents;
  • improving the product experience, conducting statistical analysis, troubleshooting, performance optimisation and feature testing;
  • complying with laws and regulations, regulatory requirements and requests of judicial or administrative authorities, and handling complaints, reports and disputes;
  • other purposes to which you have consented.

Where we use personal information for other purposes not set out in this Policy, we will obtain your consent separately or rely on another lawful basis.

3. Sensitive Personal Information

The identity documents and facial and biometric information used in real-name verification, as well as the personal information of minors under the age of fourteen, may constitute sensitive personal information. We process such information only to the extent necessary to achieve the purposes of real-name verification, age verification, anti-fraud and statutory compliance, and adopt stricter access control, encryption, de-identification and audit measures.

You understand and agree that, to complete real-name verification, we may provide your name, document type, document number, document photograph, facial image or liveness detection results to third-party identity verification providers such as Didit and receive the verification results. Third-party providers may process the above information within their independent sphere of control; please also read their privacy policies. We will not use facial information for purposes unrelated to real-name verification, nor will we use it for advertising or user profiling.

4. How We Share, Entrust the Processing of and Disclose Information

4.1 Entrusted Processing

We may entrust suppliers in areas such as cloud services, identity verification, payment, SMS/email, push notifications, content distribution, translation, customer service, data analytics and security risk control to process the necessary information. We will require them by agreement to process information in accordance with our instructions, this Policy and laws and regulations, and to adopt confidentiality and security measures.

4.2 Sharing

Except in the following circumstances, we will not share your personal information with third parties:

  • where we have obtained your explicit consent or authorization;
  • where necessary to complete a service you have actively selected (such as third-party login, payment, real-name verification or content sharing);
  • where necessary to comply with laws and regulations, regulatory requirements or mandatory requests of judicial or administrative authorities;
  • where necessary to protect the lawful rights and interests, personal safety and property safety of you, other users, the public or us;
  • in the event of a merger, acquisition, reorganization, asset transfer or similar transaction, information may be transferred as an asset, and we will require the transferee to continue to be bound by this Policy.

4.3 Public Disclosure

We will publicly disclose your personal information only with your separate consent or where required by laws and regulations. In public notices regarding non-compliant accounts, we will follow the principles of necessity and minimisation and will not display sensitive personal information unrelated to the handling of the violation.

4.4 No Sale

We do not sell your personal information, nor do we provide your personal information to third parties for profit, except as otherwise provided by laws and regulations or with your separate consent.

5. Cross-Border Transfer

Our servers and suppliers may be located outside your country/region. Where it is necessary to transfer your personal information overseas, we will, in accordance with applicable laws and regulations, carry out notification, obtain consent, conduct security assessments, enter into standard contracts or follow other statutory procedures, and will require the overseas recipient to adopt corresponding protective measures.

When you use overseas third-party services (such as overseas identity verification, cloud, payment or login services), the relevant information may be processed by that third party in its own country/region, subject to its privacy policy.

6. Storage and Protection

6.1 Storage Location

We will store the personal information of domestic users within the territory of the People's Republic of China, except as otherwise provided by law or with your separate consent. For overseas users, we may store information in their region or in the cloud service regions we use, as required for the Services.

6.2 Retention Period

We retain your personal information only for the shortest period necessary to achieve the purposes of processing. Where laws and regulations provide otherwise for retention periods, those provisions shall apply; for example, network logs, real-name verification records, transaction records, and report and penalty records may be retained for longer periods in accordance with cybersecurity, e-commerce, tax, audit or regulatory requirements. After the expiry of these periods, we will delete or anonymise the information, or apply secure isolation measures.

6.3 Security Measures

We adopt measures such as access control, least privilege, transmission encryption, storage encryption, de-identification, auditing, log retention, employee confidentiality and security training to protect personal information. The internet environment is not absolutely secure, and we cannot guarantee that information is one hundred percent secure; in the event of a personal information security incident, we will activate our emergency response plan, take remedial measures and notify you and the regulatory authorities in accordance with law.

7. Your Rights

To the extent permitted by laws and regulations, you have the right to:

  • access and copy your personal information;
  • correct and supplement inaccurate or incomplete information;
  • delete personal information or delete your account;
  • withdraw consent (without affecting processing carried out on the basis of consent before withdrawal);
  • restrict or object to specific processing, or request an explanation of the processing rules;
  • obtain a copy of your personal information or request its transfer (where the statutory conditions are met);
  • complain or report issues in the processing of personal information.

You may exercise the above rights through account settings, privacy settings, the data request page or help@sytronichub.com. To protect security, we may require you to verify your identity; we will respond within a reasonable period. We may, in accordance with law, refuse requests that are repeated without justified reason, exceed a reasonable scope or may harm the rights and interests of others, or charge a reasonable cost for them.

The specific process and consequences of account deletion are governed by the Account Deletion Agreement. After deletion, we will delete or anonymise the relevant information, except for records that must be retained by law, transactions and security records already generated, and information required for dispute resolution.

8. Cookies and Similar Technologies

We use essential cookies to maintain login and security, preference cookies to remember language, theme and playback settings, and analytics cookies to understand how features are used. You may delete or reject cookies through your browser settings; however, if essential cookies are disabled, features such as login, payment and security verification may not function properly.

We may use third-party analytics or push SDKs, which may collect device identifiers, logs and interaction information in accordance with their own privacy policies. We will require them to comply with applicable law and our security requirements.

9. Protection of Minors

We attach great importance to the protection of minors' personal information. Children under the age of fourteen shall use the Services with the consent and guidance of their guardians; we will not knowingly collect children's personal information without guardian consent.

For minors, we may restrict personalized recommendations, payments, tipping, live streaming, direct messages or access to some content, and adopt stricter review and protection measures. If a guardian discovers that a minor has provided personal information or made purchases without consent, the guardian may contact us through customer service to request deletion, correction or other handling.

10. Automated Decision-Making and Algorithmic Recommendations

We may use algorithms for content recommendation, ranking, security risk control and anti-cheating. Algorithmic recommendations are intended to improve content relevance, and you may adjust your recommendation preferences or turn off personalized recommendations through settings. We will not make decisions that have a significant impact on your rights and interests solely through automated decision-making, unless otherwise provided by law or separately agreed by you.

11. Policy Updates

We may revise this Policy based on laws and regulations, business changes or technological updates. The revised Policy will be published in the Policy Center with its effective date indicated; material changes will be notified to you through in-site announcements, pop-ups, email or other reasonable means. If you do not agree with the revisions, you should stop using the relevant features; continued use constitutes acceptance of the revised Policy.

  • In-site channels: Help Center / Privacy Settings / Data Requests
